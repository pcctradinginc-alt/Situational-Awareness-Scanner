# Thiel / Shulman adjacent strategic notes (condensed)

Paraphrased, uncertainty-tagged notes on adjacent strategic framings. Used only
to enrich the theme vector.

## Thiel-style framing: monopoly and counter-narrative
Durable value accrues to companies with a monopoly-like moat and a defensible
counter-narrative. The infrastructure provider / enabler with a real bottleneck
position is preferable to a commodity participant. Underpriced infrastructure is
the contrarian opportunity.

## Shulman-style framing: scaling and timelines
The argument emphasizes scaling laws, automation and robotics, and an AGI
timeline that pulls infrastructure demand forward. Industrial automation and
autonomous systems extend the compute and power demand curve.

## Supply-chain bottlenecks as the investable edge
Across these framings, the consistent investable edge is the supply-chain
bottleneck: semiconductor equipment, memory bandwidth, networking, power and
cooling. The picks-and-shovels suppliers benefit from cloud capex regardless of
which frontier lab wins.
