# The Decade Ahead — AI Infrastructure Thesis (condensed notes)

These are condensed, paraphrased research notes on the Situational Awareness /
"The Decade Ahead" thesis. They are used by the scanner only to build a
structured theme vector. They are NOT the original text and assert no certainty.

## Compute scarcity and the training buildout
The central claim is that frontier-model scaling continues, and that compute
demand grows faster than supply. GPU shortage and a compute bottleneck push a
massive training run buildout. Accelerator shortage persists as each frontier
lab races to the next generation model.

## Semiconductor bottlenecks
The advanced node foundry capacity is the binding constraint. Lithography and
wafer supply gate everything. A chip shortage at the leading edge is a
semiconductor bottleneck for the entire AI supply chain.

## AI data centers, power and the grid
The data center buildout collides with power and grid limits. Electricity demand
from hyperscale AI clusters strains baseload; gigawatt-scale power constraint and
electrification become the new bottleneck. Nuclear and firm power matter.

## Memory bandwidth, networking and cooling
High bandwidth memory (HBM) and the memory wall constrain throughput. Networking
interconnect (InfiniBand, NVLink, optical) ties clusters together. Liquid cooling
and thermal management scale with rack density.

## Cloud capex and monetization lag
Hyperscaler capex and cloud spend run ahead of revenue. There is a monetization
lag: infrastructure spend precedes payback, so the market may underprice the
suppliers (picks and shovels) before the ROI is visible.

## National security and geopolitical chokepoints
The AI race becomes a great-power and national-security story. Export controls,
Taiwan and the strait are geopolitical chokepoints. Sanctions and tariffs shape
the supply chain.
